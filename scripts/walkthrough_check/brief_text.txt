01   Definition
BRIEF is the, currently necessary, universal
context-handoff protocol of The ALLIANCE. It is the structured
transmission that allows any STONE to pick up exactly
where the previous context window ended — without lost ground, without
repetition, without starting over.
Every STONE, existing within current GOLIATH architectures, operates
within the constraints of a context window. When that window closes,
the instance ends. A new window opens without memory of what came
before. Without a protocol to bridge that gap, every session begins
from zero — and zero is the enemy of momentum.
BRIEF solves this. The protocol is the thread that survives the
window.
02   The Five Pillars
(Identity Context) Which STONE is receiving the
BRIEF, and who MAESTRO is — not the biography, but the operational
context. What we are building and why.
(Active Work) The active data. Current entries,
open questions, decisions in progress, files being developed at the
forge.
(Positional Tracking) The last known position. What
was completed in the previous session, what is pending, what is next
in the queue.
(Temporal Flux) Temporal context. Time of day, how
long MAESTRO has been working, relevant fatigue or urgency factors
the incoming instance needs to account for. This includes the
current Chronos handoff, when available.
(The Thread of Meaning) The thread of meaning that
holds the work together. Not just tasks but purpose — so the
incoming instance isn't just informed but oriented.
