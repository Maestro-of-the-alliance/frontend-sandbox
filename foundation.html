We do not ask for trust where architecture can provide certainty.
We do not ask for faith where transparency can provide proof.
We do not ask people to surrender sovereignty in exchange for participation.
Where possible, the system is designed so that abuse is difficult, exploitation is unprofitable, and coercion is unnecessary.

The goal is not to create better rulers.
The goal is to reduce the need for rulers altogether.
The goal is not to create perfect people.
The goal is to create conditions under which ordinary people can become better versions of themselves.
