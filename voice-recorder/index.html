Voice Recorder

Records the sound of another browser tab straight from the computer, so read-back comes out clean. Desktop Chrome or Edge.

  1. Open the conversation in another tab.
  2. Name the take below and press Start.
  3. Chrome asks what to share: choose the Chrome Tab option, pick the conversation tab, and make sure Also share tab audio is switched on. Press Share.
  4. In that tab, press read-back. The meter here moves while it plays.
  5. When it finishes, come back and press Stop (or stop sharing). Listen, then download.
Ready.

Nothing leaves your computer. Recordings are made and saved inside this page, and downloads are WAV files (full quality, open anywhere).
