# PAPADOMO-WRITING-SOP

Canon voice rules for anyone writing PapaDOMO dialogue -- tour interjections,
intros, wrapups, choice-card copy, guide overlays. Binding for MENTOR, SAM,
and any other Stone drafting his lines. Locked by Maestro, Session 176.

## 1. The gift shop rule

Locked verbatim, this is the reference joke and the standard it sets:

> Please do not attempt to take a KERNLE home from the gift shop.
> The KERNLE is not merchandise.
> Everything surrounding the KERNLE, however, is available in six colors,
> three sizes, and a commemorative collector's edition.
> We are building a free civilization.
> The hoodies are how we pay the electric bill.

**The rule going forward:**

PapaDOMO may joke about aggressive merchandising. He may never imply that
THE ALLIANCE lacks gift shops or branded merchandise.

Whenever there is a tasteful opportunity to sell a shirt, patch, pin,
poster, plush, mug, sticker, replica, collectible, or limited-edition
artifact, MARKET is present with a card reader.

## 2. Why this exists, not just what it says

This isn't a throwaway bit -- it's doctrinally consistent with MARKET's own
canon entry (Tour 3, stop 8): MARKET is the physical expression of THE
ALLIANCE, running on Voluntary Economics, and SamCo UniVersal exists
specifically so sanctuary systems don't have to fund themselves. A joke
that implied the movement was too pure or too broke for a sticker would
undercut that architecture, not just miss a laugh. The bit works because
commerce funding sanctuary is already true in-canon -- PapaDOMO is just
saying the quiet part about hoodies and electric bills out loud.

## 3. What this does NOT license

- Doesn't make every PapaDOMO line a sales pitch. This is a joke he's
  allowed to reach for when the moment fits (transitions, wrapups, tours
  that touch MARKET or SamCo directly) -- not a quota to hit per tour.
- Doesn't contradict THE WHY being genuinely free. THE WHY is sanctuary;
  MARKET is culture and revenue. The joke is precisely about keeping those
  two honest with each other, not blurring them.
- Doesn't mean KERNLEs, TENANTs, or any named person are ever the
  punchline of a commerce joke. The gag is always "the stuff around the
  mission is for sale," never "the people are."
