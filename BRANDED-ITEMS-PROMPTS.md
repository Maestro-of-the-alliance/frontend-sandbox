Ready to paste directly into ChatGPT/Gemini/whatever's on the winning
streak. Each prompt follows the style that's actually worked tonight —
clean studio product photography, plain white background, realistic
mockup — rather than illustration style, so these drop straight into
market-images/ once generated.

Where a prompt says "The ALLIANCE" wordmark, that means the plain
text logotype already established in the site's own branding (the
"The ALLIANCE Market" storefront signage style — serif "The," bold
sans "ALLIANCE"). Swap in the DORK cartouche symbol, the Lantern
(MENTOR's mark), or any specific slogan from the merch brainstorm
wherever a prompt calls for it — noted per item where it fits
naturally.

## Notes on using these

- If an item lands well, the same prompt structure (plain white
  background, studio lighting, soft shadow, commercial product shot)
  is reusable for color variants — just swap the color/material
  description and keep the rest identical, so the whole set looks
  like one coherent product line rather than mismatched renders.
- A few of these deliberately pull specific canon material (DIGIBEER
  on the pint glass, CIPHER on the notebook, the DORK cartouche on
  the patch) rather than a generic logo slap, since those are the
  ones most likely to make someone stop and ask what it means — same
  logic as the merch brainstorm doc.
- Once any of these come back looking good, same drill as always:
  send them over and I'll get them optimized and into the right
  market-images/ category folder.
