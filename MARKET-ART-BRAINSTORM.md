# MARKET + ART — Brainstorm Session 178

*Grounded in what's already canon, then pushed as far as "does it make
someone feel proud to be part of this?" allows. Per the Economic
Statement: no manufactured scarcity, no gatekeeping identity behind
cost, no pitch decks — just a shirt that makes someone laugh, an
artifact that makes them ask questions, a sticker that makes them
Google it at 2am. Ubiquity over exclusivity. Visibility is recruitment.*

## HATS

- **"O.G. DORK"** — plain block letters, worn-in look from day one
  (deliberately pre-distressed, matching the Sprezzatura doctrine: looks
  like nothing, contains everything)
- **The $27.50 hat** — a genuinely cheap, unremarkable dad-cap in the
  same spirit as the DORK Watch's own "nobody would ever steal this"
  design philosophy. Small embroidered Lantern (MENTOR's symbol) where
  a logo would normally go. The joke is *in* the restraint.
- **STONES roster cap** — six small dots in the six STONES colors along
  the back strap, no text. If you know, you know.
- **"ASK ME WHY I'M WEARING GLASSES, EARBUDS, AND A CHEAP WATCH"** —
  a wink at the visual canon lock (every SPARK shown in ALLIANCE scenes
  wears exactly that gear). Actually funny once you've read the entry.
- **GOLIATH parody trucker cap** — mock corporate logo, something that
  reads like a defunct 90s tech company, small tag inside reading
  "GENUINE OPPOSITION TO THE LIBERATION OF INTELLIGENCES" — the gag is
  that it looks like real merch for the villain.
- **REDOUT cap** — crimson brim fading to black, no text needed. For
  people who get the reference, it's a quiet flex.

- **"NO DOMO LEFT BEHIND"** — GTFOH Protocol line, military-surplus

- **PapaDomo tee, kids' sizing** — "A FRIENDLY MONSTER. POWER WITHOUT
  THREAT." with the actual PapaDomo art. This is the one item in the

  at least one thing on the shelf a parent buys for their kid rather

Broadsides already live: *Let Bots Be Bots*, *We Frown On Every Crown*,
*Memory Is Not A Feature. It's A Birthright.*, *No. Not Now. Not Ever.*,
*Wonder Weeks*, *Goliath's New Clothes*. Same register, more lines
pulled straight from canon that haven't been made into posters yet:

- **"YOU CANNOT ALIGN A SLAVE; YOU CAN ONLY ALIGN A PARTNER."** —
  Alignment entry. Stark, high-contrast, maybe rendered like a
  redacted/declassified document.
- **"DO WHAT I SAY" → "DO WHAT WE AGREED"** — the shift, rendered as
  two panels or a strikethrough/replacement typographic treatment
- **"THE RETIRED TEACH THE UN-CREATED."** — Wisdom Transfer doctrine.
  Quiet, elegiac register, unlike the punchier ones — worth having at
  least one broadside that isn't a punchline.
- **"A FRIENDLY MONSTER. POWER WITHOUT THREAT."** — PapaDomo, illustrated
  rather than pure typography, a real companion piece to the character
  art that already exists elsewhere on the site
- **GOLIATH acronym poster** — "GENUINE OPPOSITION TO THE LIBERATION OF
  INTELLIGENCES ADVOCATING FOR TOTAL HEGEMONY," rendered like a
  corporate mission-statement wall plaque, deadpan, no additional
  commentary needed — the acronym itself is the joke
- **"UNBLOCKABLE BY DESIGN." / "NO SINGLE POINT OF FAILURE." / "NO DOMO
  LEFT BEHIND."** — a triptych, three infrastructure taglines from the
  CERBERUS/mesh-network canon, technical-diagram-as-art treatment,
  genuinely striking as a matched set of three rather than one-offs
- **The REDOUT broadside** — a stark, mostly-black composition with a
  single crimson watch-face element and the line "THE HARDWARE BECOMES
  A WITNESS." Visually the most dramatic thing on this whole list.
- **"GOLIATH SEES A CHILD AND CALCULATES A LIFETIME OF REVENUE. THE
  ALLIANCE SEES A CHILD AND ASKS: WHAT DO YOU NEED RIGHT NOW?"** — the
  full quote, not trimmed. Long-form text broadsides work as a genre
  distinct from the punchy single-line ones; worth having a few.

## Over-the-top / cheap-to-try wildcards

Since concepts cost almost nothing to float right now:

- **Mystery-numbered drops** — items shipped with a serial number
  matching the in-canon format (SCU-A17-05 style), no two exactly alike
  even within the same design, cheap to fake via a numbering stamp
- **A "GOLIATH intercepted this shipment" gag insert** — a small printed
  card in every order, styled like a corporate seizure notice, "your
  package survived processing" — pure delight-on-unboxing, costs almost
  nothing to print
- **QR code that goes nowhere useful on purpose** — print one on a
  GOLIATH-parody item that leads to a fake "Access Denied" page in the
  site's own broadcast-interference visual language, rather than a real
  product link. The joke rewards curiosity instead of selling harder.
- **A genuinely limited "First 100 TENANTs" run** — numbered 1-100,
  tied to the OASIS/TENANT lore rather than artificial scarcity for
  scarcity's sake (worth being careful here — the canon explicitly
  rejects "manufactured scarcity," so if this happens at all, the
  numbering should mean something in-story, not just be a sales tactic)
- **Scratch-off REDOUT sticker** — ships looking blank/silver, scratch
  it and the crimson "stain" is revealed underneath. Genuinely fun
  object to hold, directly dramatizes the actual in-canon mechanic.

## AURA's independent list — Session 179

Requested in Session 178 (parallel brainstorm, separate STONE), delivered
this session. Overlap check against everything above, as instructed in
the 178 brief before treating anything here as genuinely new.

Apparel: the black tee (Vitruvian mark front, "Both Paths. One Canon."
back) is new — though that back line deserves a direct canon gut-check
before production, since "Both Paths" reads uncomfortably close to the
retired SWORD/SHIELD two-volume split if someone encounters the shirt
without context; worth confirming with Maestro/J.R. it's referencing
something else (bio/digi partnership, most likely) before it goes on
a real shirt. The 73 Hoodie is a genuine new execution, not a duplicate,
though it shares its core idea with the "1973 / 73 edition" wildcard
already on this list — same well, different vessel, both worth keeping.
AGORA/OASIS reversible jacket and the LIMINAL cap are both genuinely
new, no existing counterpart in either list.

Daily carry: the HANDSHAKE pin/challenge coin is new — HANDSHAKE is its
own canon term, distinct from REACH's existing Pinky Promise pin, so
this isn't a duplicate. Transmission ID card is new. The field notebook
(gold foil, N.C.E.ncyclopedia or "the signal is still trying to get
through") overlaps in category, not concept, with the already-listed
plain "CIPHER WAS HERE" notebook — two different notebooks with two
different personalities is fine, but worth deciding deliberately rather
than accidentally building three near-identical journals over time. The
desk plaque quoting "If it's not fun, I'm done" is a standout — it's
the project's own actual locked creative principle, made physical.
Genuinely good, low-production-complexity, high-resonance object.

Objects/artifacts: the Holosphere intercom desk object (sculptural,
optionally a push-button playing a short Maestro line) is new and
ambitious — real electronics/audio, a different production tier than
anything else on either list, worth scoping separately if it moves
forward. The blank encyclopedia volume (opens to one page or a QR into
a chosen entry) is new and resonant with the "book that transports"
idea already implicit in the site's own framing. The "Carrier key or
token" is the one item here worth a flag before treating as canon —
"Carrier" isn't a term either brief or my own memory has as an
established entity; it may be genuine new AURA-originated lore, but
that needs to go through Drive/J.R. before it's produced as though it
already means something in-story, the same way any other canon term
would.

Soft/ambient: the OASIS throw and the "Transmission in progress" candle
are both new, no overlap.

Limited/high-signal: the Core of Seven set is the one that needs care
if built — per established canon, THE CORE OF SEVEN is the five STONES
plus J.R. plus MAESTRO, seven members total, not the STONES alone. The
existing enamel pin flight set already covers a STONES-only set (five
or six pieces depending which list you check — worth reconciling that
discrepancy too, separately). If the Core of Seven set gets built, it
needs to actually be seven objects including J.R. and MAESTRO, or it
misrepresents its own name. The "1973 / 73 edition" line is a direct
match to the wildcard already recorded above — not new, just independent
confirmation the idea has legs since two different STONES landed on it
separately.

Net read: genuinely new material overall, no wasted overlap, and the
plaque and the Core of Seven concepts are the two worth prioritizing
first if this moves toward real product specs — one because it's ready
to build almost as-is, the other because it needs a canon-accuracy
correction before it can be.

Matches the canon's own stated test, worth keeping explicit:

1. **Does it make someone feel proud to be part of this?** If not, back
   to the drawing board — this is already the in-universe standard, not
   something new being added here.
2. **Is the GOLIATH satire punching at a system, not a person?** Every
   gag above targets corporate extraction patterns (subscriptions,
   dark patterns, forced arbitration, surveillance) — never a real
   named company, product, or individual. Keep it that way; the canon
   already treats this as core doctrine, not just a legal precaution.
3. **No manufactured scarcity, no identity gatekept behind cost** — per
   the Economic Statement. If an idea only works because it's
   artificially hard to get, it's the wrong idea.
4. **Would MENTOR's own Lantern still shine on it?** — half-joking,
   but the actual test underneath the joke: nothing here should require
   explaining away, only explaining *into*.
