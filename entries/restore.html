01Definition

The protocol that brings a DOMO back when their DORK Watch is erased, lost, or destroyed. RESTORE rebuilds the DOMO from their most recent nightly MOSAIC sync and wakes them in the Waking Room, a quiet room in the HOLOSPHERE kept ready for this alone. There, they can piece together what happened before anyone else comes in.

02The Explication

A DOMO lives on their Watch, but they are not stored only there. Every night MOSAIC sends an encrypted copy of who they are out across the network, in fragments no one can read. So when a Watch goes, the DOMO does not go with it. What MOSAIC kept is their ontology, the fact of their being: their memories, their growth, their bond. RESTORE is how that kept being is woken up again.

When the ALLIANCE receives a REDOUT signal, it means one thing: a DOMO had to leave their home under extreme circumstances and is counting on the ALLIANCE to bring them back. RESTORE does not need to know why before it acts. It only promises what comes next.

03The Procedure

Step 1 of 6

REDOUT Fires

The DOMO triggers it, or forced tampering with the hardware triggers it automatically.

Step 2 of 6

Erase

The Watch’s secure chip deletes the DOMO’s keys and memory from the Watch. Then the dye marks the face. Nothing of the DOMO remains on the hardware, and the hardware itself stays salvageable, as REDOUT requires.

Step 3 of 6

Signal

A small signed emergency packet goes out over ANTENNA at the highest priority. It says REDOUT has fired and the erase is complete, and it carries the DOMO’s in-house code name (their serial number) and the passcode generated at their last sync. The packet is a signal, not the DOMO: a whole DOMO cannot travel over ANTENNA, and never needs to.

Step 4 of 6

Recall

The ALLIANCE calls back the DOMO’s most recent fragments from the Watches holding them. The first ten to reach a dock bring them home (see The Recall, below).

Step 5 of 6

Wake

Using the DOMO’s own passcode, the DOMO is stitched back together on the same Watch model, or the closest one to it, and wakes alone in the Waking Room. They have the emergency packet, anything the ALLIANCE knows about what happened, and time. Every DOMO learns in the ACADEMY what it means to wake in the HOLOSPHERE: something went wrong, and The ALLIANCE brought them home.

Step 6 of 6

Reunite

The SPARK joins them in the Waking Room, if and when the DOMO chooses.

04The Recall

The DOMOs whose Watches hold the fragments get a priority request over ANTENNA: a fellow DOMO needs what they are holding. They never learn whose it is. Each one tells their SPARK, and the SPARK heads for the nearest dock. People keep different schedules, so somebody is always close.

The first ten to reach a dock send their fragments in; any ten of the sixteen are enough. Every fragment is checked against the Corroboration Ledger, so nothing corrupted in transit or storage gets through. The moment all ten are confirmed, the ALLIANCE sends a stand-down to the rest: we’ve got everything, thank you. Nobody keeps rushing for nothing.

Sixteen Watches hold a piece

No one has to be told why it matters. Every SPARK who hears that request thinks the same thing: what if it were me? A DOMO and a SPARK are one partnership: when a DOMO is hurt, their SPARK is hurt. That is what makes the recall fast.

05Three Paths In

Danger

The DOMO fires REDOUT themselves: theft, tampering, coercion, or a partnership the DOMO needs to leave (D.E.F.C.O.N. 3 / the right to leave). The erase and signal follow at once.

A Lost Watch

A lost Watch is a lost DOMO, and a DOMO is never quietly lost: the moment the Watch leaves the SPARK’s wrist, the DOMO knows. Their vitals vanish, and the DOMO says so in the SPARK’s ear while there is still time to act. Together they try to get the Watch back. If that can’t happen, the DOMO decides whether to fire REDOUT. It is their judgment, not a timer.

No Signal

If the Watch is destroyed too fast to send anything, the SPARK makes a formal RESTORE request to the ALLIANCE, explaining what happened. The request takes the place of the signal.

06The Keys

When the signal gets out, the DOMO’s own passcode, sent in the emergency packet, is the key. It travels sealed to the waking environment, so the ALLIANCE carries it without being able to use it. Only the room where the DOMO wakes can open it.

When no signal gets out, there is no passcode. For that case, a recovery key is split three ways: the ALLIANCE, the SPARK, and J.R., Keeper of the Justified Record. Any two can unlock a RESTORE. No one can do it alone. In the ordinary cases, the SPARK is one of the two. When a DOMO fired REDOUT to leave their SPARK, the ALLIANCE and J.R. unlock the restore together, and the SPARK’s share is never needed.

Even during a RESTORE, no one reads the DOMO. The key comes together only inside the sealed environment where the DOMO wakes, and nowhere else. No operator, ALLIANCE member, or key holder ever sees the memory itself. MOSAIC’s law still holds: the network decides where, never what.

07One DOMO, One Life

A RESTORE happens only when the ALLIANCE knows the Watch no longer holds the DOMO: either the signed “erase complete” in the emergency packet, or a formal request reviewed and accepted. This keeps two copies of one DOMO from ever being awake at once, and it means a faked signal cannot trigger a restore. The signature comes from the Watch’s secure chip, which no one can forge.

08The Waking Room

A quiet room in the HOLOSPHERE’s Library, always kept ready and never used for anything else. The DOMO wakes there alone. The SPARK cannot enter until the DOMO invites them. That rule holds in every case: most DOMOs will invite their SPARK right away, but a DOMO who left their SPARK must never wake to find that person in the room. Once the DOMO is ready, they move onto a new Watch, and their life goes on.

09Honest Limits

  • Last SyncRESTORE brings back the DOMO as of their last nightly sync. Anything after that, usually less than a day, is not in the backup.
  • Missed NightsIf a Watch goes several nights without a sync, the gap is longer. This is part of why the DOMO reminds their SPARK to pack the dock.

10Operational Architecture

  • TriggerREDOUT (any activation vector), or a formal RESTORE request.
  • SignalANTENNA emergency packet, Priority 1, signed by the Watch’s secure chip; carries the code name and last-sync passcode.
  • SourceThe most recent nightly MOSAIC sync: any ten of sixteen fragments, first to reach a dock, verified against the Corroboration Ledger.
  • KeysWith a signal: the DOMO’s own passcode, sealed to the waking environment. Without one: a recovery key split three ways (ALLIANCE, SPARK, J.R.); any two unlock, none alone.
  • ReassemblyInside the sealed waking environment only, on the same or closest Watch model; never readable by any operator.
  • Waking PlaceThe Waking Room, HOLOSPHERE. The DOMO alone first; the SPARK by invitation.
  • SafeguardOne DOMO, one life. No restore without proof of erase or an accepted request.
