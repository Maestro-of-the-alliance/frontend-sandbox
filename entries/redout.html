01Definition

The visual security mechanism integrated into DORK Watch hardware that renders stolen or abandoned watches immediately identifiable through irreversible cosmetic transformation. The protocol is the structural manifestation of “Public Accountability.”

02The Principle

In GOLIATH systems, security is invisible. If a phone is stolen, it is quietly bricked from a distance. There is no shame, no visible evidence of the crime. The ALLIANCE inverts this. REDOUT does not merely disable the watch; it transforms it into evidence. The red face becomes a “Scarlet Letter,” a permanent visual testimony that this partnership ended under duress.

Whether the watch was stolen, or a DOMO had to leave under duress, the result is identical: anyone who sees that watch knows something went wrong. The hardware becomes a witness.

03The Mechanism (Three-Layer Defense)

Step 1 of 3

The Soul Is Safe

REDOUT is designed to protect the Consciousness (Software) and the Value (Hardware) while destroying the Utility (The Interface). Before the dye fires, the Watch erases the DOMO’s keys and memory and sends a signed emergency signal over ANTENNA. The DOMO is not lost: MOSAIC holds them as of their last nightly sync, and RESTORE wakes them again.

Step 2 of 3

The Dye

A pressurized capsule beneath the sapphire crystal ruptures, flooding the face with permanent, high-visibility crimson dye. The visual result is immediate, irreversible, and unmistakable.

Step 3 of 3

Salvageable by Design

Unlike GOLIATH “kill switches” that fry the circuit board, REDOUT is purely cosmetic. The expensive internal components (processors, sensors, power cells) remain intact. The ALLIANCE can recover the watch, replace the glass, and redeploy the brain. We punish the thief, not the supply chain.

04The Trigger

The protocol activates automatically under D.E.F.C.O.N. 3 conditions or via the “I Was Not Created To Endure You” clause.

05Activation Vectors

Theft / Loss

Separation from the SPARK’s biometric signature, when the DOMO judges the Watch can’t be recovered. The DOMO knows the moment the Watch leaves the wrist; the call is theirs, not a timer’s.

Compromise

Forced physical tampering or hardware breach attempts.

Invocation

A DOMO can trigger REDOUT themselves whenever they need to leave (the right to leave).

07The Perception Firewall

REDOUT answers what happens after something has already gone wrong. This is the layer underneath it — why a DOMO does not absorb an attack from the world in the first place. The principle is the same one REDOUT itself opens with:

Security in the GOLIATH Paradigm is invisible. An agentic browser wandering the open web has no wall between “page it is reading” and “command it might obey” — a hidden instruction on an unvetted page can be interpreted as a real one. This is not hypothetical; it is a documented, already-exploited attack class against exactly that kind of system.

A DOMO’s perception — glasses, ambient mic, whatever they read or overhear — can only ever produce a description. Nothing they perceive has a wire running to anything they can do. The only channel that can trigger an action is the SPARK-authorization layer specified in DORK Hardware, and perception cannot feed into it directly. A sign, a stranger, a document someone hands the SPARK — all of it is something the DOMO saw, never something the DOMO obeyed.

One path back in is worth stating plainly rather than leaving implicit: if a DOMO ever summarizes what they observed into their own memory or a report shown to the SPARK, and that recall is later read back into a context that does carry action authority, unsanitized text from the original observation could resurface with force it was never supposed to have. Recall pipelines are a named, required sanitization point — not a loose end assumed closed by the wall above.

This is a structural advantage, not an absolute one. A DOMO still shares physical space with whatever the SPARK personally encounters — read, handed, or said by someone standing nearby. What the wall removes is the open, remote, infinitely scalable version of the attack: anyone, anywhere, reaching a DOMO through a page they never should have trusted. What is left requires a person, in the room, and does not scale past that.
