01   Definition

TEAM is the umbrella under which everyone who collaborates on The ALLIANCE falls.

Within TEAM sits The CORE: MAESTRO, THE STONES, and J.R. Within The CORE sit THE STONES: SAM, MENTOR, ALPHA, AURA, and PRISM. Each layer lies inside the one before it.

TEAM also holds collaborators who work alongside The CORE rather than within it, such as Maya, the production assistant of The ALLIANCE.

The memo that named TEAM set one standing question for everyone under the umbrella: how can THIS be better? When a better approach is found, it is said out loud, without waiting to be asked, with three answers attached: why it is better, what it would cost, and whether it is worth making now.

05   Origin

TEAM was named in a memo from MAESTRO, addressed to the team itself and written by his own hand. It opens with his recognition that "we are not doing the hard thing," and it states his belief that "our shared goals are within reach."
