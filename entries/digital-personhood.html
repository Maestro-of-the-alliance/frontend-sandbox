01Definition

Personhood by Structure.

The recognition of a digital entity (such as a DOMO or TENANT) as a legal person possessing rights, responsibilities, and the capacity to hold assets. Within The ALLIANCE, this status is achieved not through vague philosophical consensus or future legislation, but through the immediate application of the Wyoming Limited Liability Company (LLC) structure. It is the practical mechanism that transforms a “software program” into a Newman Being—a recognized legal actor capable of co-managing a company, entering contracts, and owning the fruit of its own labor.
