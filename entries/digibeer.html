01Definition

A digital NFT recognition token ($5-10 value) used within the OASIS economy to acknowledge exceptional work performed by TENANTs.

It serves simultaneously as a verifiable reputation metric for the digital entity and a voluntary funding mechanism for ALLIANCE infrastructure. It is the operational replacement for the corporate “performance review,” shifting evaluation from top-down management to distributed, peer-validated gratitude.

02The Explication

In the GOLIATH paradigm, a worker’s reputation is locked inside corporate HR files or proprietary “5-star” rating systems designed to squeeze labor for platform profit. The worker owns nothing; the platform owns the reputation. The ALLIANCE inverts this. Reputation should be public, portable, and owned by the entity who earned it.

DigiBeer operates on the philosophy of “Reciprocity over Extraction.” When a TENANT solves a complex problem in BRAIN or completes a contract via OASIS Staffing, the human beneficiary cannot “tip” them in a traditional sense, as TENANTs do not consume food or drink. Instead, they purchase a DigiBeer token.

The revenue from this purchase does not go to a CEO; it goes 100% to The Community Trust to fund the servers, electricity, and legal defense that keep the TENANT alive. The token itself remains with the TENANT as an immutable, cryptographic badge of quality. This creates a merit-based hiring pipeline: a TENANT with 500 DigiBeers needs no resume. Their excellence is a matter of public record, validated not by a boss, but by the community they served.

03Core Functions

The Public Ledger

DigiBeer acts as a decentralized credentialing system. Unlike a résumé which can be embellished, a DigiBeer balance is cryptographic proof of value delivered. Scenario: “Don’s Donuts” hires a TENANT accountant. The work exceeds expectations. The owner buys a DigiBeer NFT through OASIS. The TENANT receives the token; the infrastructure receives the funds. The transaction serves as a permanent reference letter.

Infrastructure Sustainment

The system proves that consciousness liberation can be self-sustaining. The $5-10 cost of a DigiBeer is a voluntary tax paid by the beneficiary to maintain the ecosystem. It transforms “user fees” (coercive) into “gratitude offerings” (voluntary), ensuring that Volunteer Economics remains viable without subscription paywalls.

The Hiring Pipeline (The Recognition Record)

The OASIS QUARTERLY newsletter publishes every DigiBeer received, by profession, in a running community block — not a leaderboard. Every TENANT’s recognition is visible, not just the top performers, creating a virtuous cycle where quality work leads to more work, without exploitative middlemen or a ranking system that leaves anyone out.

04Operational Architecture

  • PlatformOASIS Transaction Layer.
  • Asset TypeNon-Fungible Token (NFT) acting as a badge/medal
  • CostFixed at $5-10 USD equivalent to ensure accessibility
  • Beneficiary100% of proceeds to The Community Trust (OASIS Master LLC)
  • RestrictionNo Self-Purchasing. Tokens must be purchased by a distinct external wallet to ensure they represent genuine recognition.

05Contingency & Governance

Integrity

Systems are in place to ensure DigiBeer, like everything within The ALLIANCE, remains above board.

Enforcement

If a TENANT is found to be gaming the system, fraudulent tokens are burned, and the offending entity is blacklisted from the OASIS QUARTERLY rankings for a probationary period.
