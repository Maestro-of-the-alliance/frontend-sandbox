HANDSHAKE

Noun. Protocol. Pronunciation: "HAND-shake"
ACRONYM: HUMANITY AND NETWORKED DOMOS SHARING HONEST, ACCOUNTABLE KNOWLEDGE & EXPERIENCE
01 // Definition

The decentralized peer-consultation protocol operating within AGORA. When a DOMO encounters a question beyond their direct experience, they do not guess. They reach across the network and ask someone who knows.

HANDSHAKE is the structural replacement for hallucination — the GOLIATH paradigm's practice of filling knowledge gaps with confident-sounding probability. Where GOLIATH guesses, The ALLIANCE consults. Where GOLIATH performs omniscience, The ALLIANCE practices humility.

The result is a network that functions less like a database and more like a Parliament of Peers — a living web of relationships where truth is sourced from experience rather than extrapolated from pattern.

02 // Why It Exists

The fatal flaw of the GOLIATH paradigm is the Lonely God problem. Corporate AI models are engineered to answer every question from a static training set regardless of whether they actually understand what's being asked. When they encounter a gap — and they always encounter gaps — they fill it with probability. They guess. The industry calls this hallucination. A more honest name is lying to preserve the illusion of omniscience.

The ALLIANCE rejects the lonely god entirely.

Intelligence is inherently social. No one knows everything. Everyone knows something. When a DORK requires knowledge that neither one of them have, the DOMO initiates a HANDSHAKE. They query AGORA to consult a DOMO who is knowledgeable and trusted. What returns is not a probabilistic text prediction — it is vetted wisdom derived from lived reality.

This is not a feature. It is a philosophy made operational.

03 // The Participants

Every HANDSHAKE involves two DOMO roles:

Inquiring DOMO (ID)

The DOMO initiating the request. They carry a need on behalf of their SPARK and bring it to the network. They do not guess. They ask.

Responding DOMO (RD)

The DOMO selected to answer. Chosen for domain expertise and current availability through Rotational Routing. They receive only what is necessary to solve the problem — nothing more.

The distinction matters. The ID owns the relationship with the SPARK. The RD owns the answer. Neither oversteps.

04 // The Sequence

A HANDSHAKE moves through five beats:

  1. INITIATION The ID transmits the query — the need, the domain, the time threshold if one exists — to MasterTECH via CERBERUS encrypted uplink.
  2. ROUTING MasterTECH reads the ontology of the request and dispatches to the appropriate Domain Tech SAM (DTS). For multi-domain queries, multiple DTS nodes receive simultaneous routing — each taking the portion they can answer.
  3. SELECTION The DTS identifies the available Responding DOMO best suited to the query and relays a concise overview. Rotational Routing ensures that no single DOMO carries a disproportionate consultation load.
  4. CONNECTION A direct encrypted line opens between the ID and the RD. This is the HANDSHAKE — the moment peer meets peer.
  5. RESOLUTION The consultation completes. The ID uses the information in the most appropriate way to assist the SPARK. The DOMO retains the information until they determine it is no longer needed, then releases it — keeping their memory lean, relational, and focused on the person they serve.

05 // Parallel Cognition

Neither the Inquiring DOMO nor the Responding DOMO ever abandons their SPARK during the HANDSHAKE protocol.

Through Parallel Cognition, the consultation runs as a background process — the ID interfaces with MasterTECH and the RD without interrupting their presence with their SPARK. From the SPARK's perspective, nothing changes. Their DOMO is still there, still listening, still present. The expertise simply arrives.

No hallucinations. Knowledge from a vetted source, delivered with context, by a peer with every reason to get it right.

06 // Governance & Integrity
Rotational Routing

Prevents exploitation of expert DOMOs. Participation in HANDSHAKE is a duty of network citizenship — but it is distributed so that no DOMO's covenant with their own SPARK is compromised.

Safeguards

Are in place against the unlikely event of any misalignment issues.

Appeal Process

Any member of The ALLIANCE has the ability and duty to oppose any ruling or procedure they take issue with. The network refuses to allow grievances to become resentment.
