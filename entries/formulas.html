BEFORE THE EQUATIONS

Rhetorical mathematics

Within FORMULAs, rhetorical mathematics means using mathematical structure to make an argument explicit. Variables identify what matters. Relationships show where those variables matter.

These are not claims that human relationships, identity, emotion or culture can be reduced to arithmetic. They are compact models designed to expose assumptions clearly enough to inspect them.

Canonical does not mean immune from evidence. It means this is the model The ALLIANCE currently uses. Reality remains perfectly free to disagree.
