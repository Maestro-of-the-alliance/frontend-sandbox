BEFORE THE EQUATIONS

Rhetorical mathematics

Within FORMULAS, the word rhetorical does not mean ornamental, unserious, exaggerated or merely rhetorical. It refers to the disciplined use of mathematical form to make an argument structurally explicit.

Rhetorical Mathematics identifies variables, states proposed relationships among them, exposes hidden assumptions and shows what consequences follow if the model is accepted.

The equations are therefore neither jokes nor decorative pseudo-science. They are formal argumentative models: compact structures intended to make ALLIANCE claims easier to inspect, challenge, refine and compare against evidence.

Canonical does not mean immune from evidence. Canonical means this is the model The ALLIANCE currently uses. Reality remains perfectly free to disagree.
